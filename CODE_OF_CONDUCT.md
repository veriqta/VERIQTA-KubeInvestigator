# Community conduct

Participate respectfully and constructively. Welcome people of different experience levels and backgrounds. Critique technical work without personal attacks. Harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing private information and unwanted contact are unacceptable.

Repository maintainers may remove abusive material, limit participation or close discussions that violate these expectations. Decisions should be proportionate to conduct and protect participant privacy. No independent enforcement team or private contact is claimed; owner setup must establish a private conduct-reporting route before it is advertised. Do not post personal or sensitive incident details publicly. Where immediate safety is at risk, use appropriate local assistance.
