# Roadmap

The authoritative first-release gates are in [release readiness](docs/release-readiness.md): separately authorised live evaluation and human review, runtime-maintainer assignment, a verified private security-reporting route, independently trusted release signing/provenance review, and explicit distribution/visibility approval. Real-cluster browser, optional evidence, bounded-load and recorded native architecture/version checks are implemented and have scoped evidence.

Optional later improvements include deeper workload navigation, broader diagnostic cases, EndpointSlice readiness comparisons, individual identity integration, managed-platform validation and sustained capacity measurement. Cross-release schema migrations require explicit new compatibility evidence. High availability, autonomous remediation and multi-cluster control are outside the first-release contract. No future feature is advertised as implemented.
